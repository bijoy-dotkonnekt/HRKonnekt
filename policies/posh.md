# POSH Summary

## 1. About this summary
This is a short summary of DotKonnekt's prevention of sexual harassment (POSH) policy for the demo. It is not legal text. The full policy and the law apply.

## 2. Internal Committee
DotKonnekt has an Internal Committee (IC) that receives and handles complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace.

## 3. How to raise a complaint
Raise a complaint in writing to the Internal Committee or to HR. HRKonnekt does not handle complaints itself; it passes your message privately to the HR team.

## 4. Confidentiality
Complaints and the identity of the people involved are kept confidential.
