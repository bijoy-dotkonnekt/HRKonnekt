# Reimbursement Policy

## 1. What can be claimed
You can claim travel for client visits, approved certifications, internet up to ₹1,000 per month, and team lunches approved by your manager.

## 2. How to claim
Submit claims in the HRMS within 30 days of the expense, with the bill attached. Your manager approves, then Finance pays with the next salary.

## 3. Travel
Economy air travel or AC train travel is allowed for client visits. Local cab rides for client visits are reimbursed at actuals.

## 4. Limits
Claims above ₹10,000 need approval from the department head before the expense.
