# Code of Conduct

## 1. Respect
Treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

## 2. Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager and HR about any outside work, investment or relationship that could conflict with your role at DotKonnekt.

## 3. Confidential information
Do not share customer data, source code or financial information outside the company without approval.

## 4. Gifts
You may accept gifts worth up to ₹2,000 from clients or vendors. Declare anything above that to HR.

## 5. Reporting concerns
Report concerns to your manager, to HR, or through the confidential HR channel. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not allowed.
