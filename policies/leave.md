# Leave Policy

## 1. Leave year
The leave year at DotKonnekt runs from 1 January to 31 December. Quotas are credited on 1 January, except earned leave, which accrues monthly.

## 2. Casual leave
Every employee gets 12 days of casual leave (CL) per leave year. Casual leave is meant for short personal needs and can be taken for half or full days. Casual leave cannot be carried forward; unused casual leave lapses on 31 December and is not encashed.

## 3. Sick leave
Every employee gets 12 days of sick leave (SL) per leave year. A medical note is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## 4. Earned leave
Earned leave (EL) accrues at 1.25 days per month, which is 15 days a year. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward into the next leave year. Earned leave beyond 30 days can be encashed at the end of the year.

## 5. Carry forward
Only earned leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum of 30 days. Casual leave and sick leave lapse on 31 December. The carried-forward balance appears as the opening balance in the new leave year.

## 6. Applying for leave
Apply for leave in the HRMS or by asking HRKonnekt in chat. Your manager approves or rejects the request. Apply at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Weekends and company holidays inside a leave period are not counted.

## 7. Comp-off
If you work on a weekend or holiday with your manager's approval, you earn one comp-off day. Comp-off must be used within 30 days.

## 8. Loss of pay
Leave taken without enough balance is recorded as loss of pay (LOP) and is deducted in the payslip for that month.

## 9. Parental leave
Maternity leave and paternity leave (5 working days) are available. Speak to HR for eligibility and documents. These figures are illustrative for this demo and must be checked against current law.
