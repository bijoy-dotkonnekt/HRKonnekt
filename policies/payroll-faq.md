# Payroll FAQ

## 1. Payday
Salary is paid on the 30th of every month, or the last working day before it.

## 2. Payslips
Payslips are available in the HRMS under Payslips by the 1st of the next month. HRKonnekt can show you a payslip summary card, but the figures come straight from the HRMS.

## 3. Salary structure
Your monthly pay is made of basic pay, house rent allowance (HRA) and a special allowance. Deductions include provident fund (PF), professional tax and income tax (TDS). All figures in this demo are illustrative.

## 4. Tax declarations
Submit your investment declarations in the HRMS by 15 January, with proofs, so TDS can be adjusted.

## 5. Payroll questions
For a question about your salary amount, contact HR. HRKonnekt passes pay questions to the HR team.
