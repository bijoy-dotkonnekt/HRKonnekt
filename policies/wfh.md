# Work From Home Policy

## 1. Eligibility
All confirmed employees can work from home up to 2 days a week after agreeing it with their manager. Employees on probation need manager approval for each WFH day.

## 2. Marking WFH
Mark WFH days in the HRMS attendance screen before 10:30 am on that day.

## 3. Equipment and security
Use your DotKonnekt laptop and the company VPN. Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## 4. Working from another city
Working from another city for more than one week needs written approval from your manager and HR.
