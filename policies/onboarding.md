# Onboarding Guide

## 1. First week
In your first week you get your laptop, accounts and a buddy from your team. HR will walk you through the HRMS.

## 2. Probation
Probation lasts 6 months from your joining date. Your manager confirms your role at the end of probation.

## 3. One-month check-in
After 30 days, HRKonnekt sends you a short check-in so HR can hear how your first month went.

## 4. Interest groups
Join interest groups such as badminton, cricket, running or the book club by asking HRKonnekt.
