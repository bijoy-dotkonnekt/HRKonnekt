# Holiday Policy

## 1. Company holidays
DotKonnekt publishes its holiday list every December for the next calendar year. National holidays apply to all offices; some festival holidays apply only to a specific office.

## 2. Optional holidays
Each employee may take 2 optional holidays a year from the optional list, such as New Year's Day.

## 3. Working on a holiday
If you work on a company holiday with manager approval, you earn a comp-off day, which must be used within 30 days.
